Gotcha: FreshnessMetric (evaluated alongside CompletenessMetric on every round)
still fails even after Alice answers -- it scores off `modified`/`created`
timestamps that only show up in query_results_metadata when the KB answer-agent
happens to call get_metadata for that lookup, which it doesn't always do. With no
timestamps at all, freshness scores 0.0 and can never pass, regardless of what the
user says. In a real deployment with max_interaction_rounds > 1 this can burn every
remaining round on an unresolvable metric even after the metric that actually
needed the user's input has already passed.
